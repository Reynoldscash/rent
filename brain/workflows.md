# Workflows

## Inspection Workflow

### Before Pickup
Owner records:
- Walkaround video
- Photos
- Scratches / dents / damage
- Mileage / hours
- Fuel / battery
- Accessories
- Serial number
- Condition notes

Renter receives digital report and confirms.

### After Return
Owner repeats inspection.
System compares before vs after.

---

## Damage Claim Workflow

1. Owner submits claim.
2. System freezes relevant records.
3. Owner uploads evidence:
   - Photos
   - Video
   - Description
   - Repair estimate
   - Pre-rental condition proof
4. Renter notified.
5. Renter responds.
6. Platform reviews evidence.
7. Additional documentation requested if needed.
8. Deposit payment processed.
9. Appeal window.
10. Final resolution.
